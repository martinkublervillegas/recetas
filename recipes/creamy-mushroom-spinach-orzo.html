Creamy Mushroom and Spinach Orzo (Risoni)

Time 30 min
Serves 4
Source Nagi Maehashi

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 600 g (1.2 lb) mushrooms, sliced (I used button mushrooms)

Orzo / Risoni

  • 3 tbsp butter, unsalted
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 1½ cups (375 ml) milk (I used low fat)
  • 3 cups (750 ml) chicken broth
  • 1½ cups orzo / risoni, uncooked
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Black pepper
  • ¾ cup parmesan cheese, grated
  • 250 g (8 oz) frozen spinach (1 box), thawed, excess water squeezed out

Garnish

  • Extra parmesan cheese (optional)
  • Finely chopped parsley (optional)

Method

1
Heat the olive oil in a large non stick pan over high heat (if you aren't using non stick, use more oil).
2
Add the mushrooms and cook until softened (around 3 minutes). Remove from the pan and set aside.
3
Lower the heat to medium and melt the butter in the pan. Add garlic and onion, and sauté until the onion becomes translucent — around 3 minutes.
4
Add the flour, stir it in and cook for 1 minute.
5
Add the milk and chicken broth and use a whisk to mix the butter flour mixture into the liquid.
6
Increase heat to medium high. Add the orzo/risoni, salt and pepper, stir once, then put a lid on. Bring it to a simmer, then leave it to cook for around 8 to 10 minutes or until the orzo/risoni is cooked but still slightly firm, and it is still quite saucy. If you have a strong stove, turn it down slightly so it just simmers.
7
While the orzo is cooking, break up the spinach into little pieces with your fingers.
8
Remove the pan from the stove. Stir through the parmesan, spinach and cooked mushrooms. Check if more salt or pepper is required.
9
Serve immediately, garnished with more parmesan and fresh finely chopped parsley, if desired.

Notes

  • Orzo / risoni is a pasta in the shape of rice, found in the pasta section of supermarkets. It costs around the same as ordinary pasta.
  • No lid for your fry pan? Use the lid of a pot larger than the pan, or a large baking tray.
  • Stretching it — add more vegetables: extra mushrooms, or other vegetables (even frozen bags of chopped vegetables, heated through). 1½ cups of extra cooked mushrooms / spinach stretches it to serve 5 and brings it down from about 540 to around 440 calories per serving.

A one-pan, meat-free weeknight dinner that turns out creamy without any cream. Frozen spinach is the deliberate choice here: the same amount of cooked-down fresh spinach would take about five bunches. Serve it straight away — the orzo keeps soaking up the sauce as it sits.
