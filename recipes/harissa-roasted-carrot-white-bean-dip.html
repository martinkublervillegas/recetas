Harissa-Roasted Carrot and White Bean Dip

Time ~35 min
Serves ~2 cups
Source Joe Yonan

Ingredients

  • ½ lb (225 g) carrots, cut into 1-inch (2.5 cm) pieces
  • 1 tbsp harissa, plus more (optional) for finishing the dish
  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for finishing the dish
  • ½ tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 5 garlic cloves, unpeeled
  • 1¾ cups cooked or canned no-salt-added white beans (from one 15 oz / 425 g can), drained but not rinsed (reserve the liquid)
  • ¼ cup lightly packed mint leaves, plus more chopped mint for finishing the dish
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice

Method

1
Preheat the oven to 450°F (230°C).
2
On a small rimmed baking sheet, toss the carrots with the harissa, 1 tbsp of the olive oil, and the salt. Place the garlic on one side of the sheet. Roast until the carrots are fork-tender, 20 to 25 minutes. Let cool slightly.
3
Squeeze the soft garlic cloves out of their skins into the bowl of a food processor or blender. Add the carrots and use a spatula to scrape in as much of the browned roasted harissa bits from the pan as possible. Reserving a few whole beans for garnish if desired, add the rest to the food processor or blender, plus the remaining 2 tbsp of olive oil, mint, and lemon juice and puree until smooth, scraping down the sides of the bowl as needed. Add a little water or some of the reserved bean liquid, a few tablespoons at a time, to loosen the mixture if it is too pasty in texture. Taste and add more salt if needed.
4
To serve, spoon the dip onto a shallow plate and use the back of a spoon to swirl it. Add the whole beans and a little dollop or two of harissa, if desired, drizzle with olive oil, and sprinkle with the chopped mint. Serve with pitas, crackers, or raw vegetables.

Notes

  • Use cannellini, navy, great Northern, or another white bean.
  • Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 1 week.

The combination of harissa and mint—one spicy, one cooling—takes this dip into can't-stop-eating territory.
