Scallion Oil Noodles with Honey Soy Chicken

Time 30 min
Serves 2

Ingredients

Scallion oil sauce

  • 6 scallions
  • 30 g avocado oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp black vinegar
  • 15 g honey

Honey soy chicken

  • 400 g 5% chicken mince
  • 1 tsp avocado oil
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 15 g honey
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine

Noodles & finish

  • 400 g frozen ramen noodles (e.g. Yutaka)
  • 200 g Chinese broccoli or choy sum
  • Fresh spring onion, to finish
  • 2 egg yolks (optional)

Method

1
Bring a pan of salted water to the boil.
2
Cut 4 scallions into 1-inch pieces and add to a sauce pan with the avocado oil. Set over low/medium heat until beginning to char. Add the soy sauce, dark soy and honey and simmer for 1 minute. Turn the heat off and add the vinegar, set aside. Finely slice the remaining spring onions and chop the broccoli into bite-sized pieces, set aside.
3
Heat a large frying pan or wok over medium heat. Once hot, pour in the avocado oil. Add the chicken mince and cook over medium-high heat until it takes on some colour. Reduce the heat and add the dark soy, honey and grated garlic; reduce in the pan for a minute or two until glossy. Deglaze with the Shaoxing wine, remove from the pan and set aside.
4
Blanch the greens for 2 minutes in the boiling water and set aside. Cook the noodles in the same pan, drain and add to the chicken pan. Pour over the scallion oil sauce and toss until evenly coated. If too sticky, add a little noodle water.
5
Divide the noodles between two bowls, top with the chicken, chopped Chinese broccoli and a heap of sliced spring onions. Add an egg yolk if using. Mix in the bowl and enjoy.
